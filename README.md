1. Introduction
Spoons3D ("we", "us", or "our") developed Learning with Rory ("the app") as a children’s educational application designed for young children aged 2 to 5 years and their parents or guardians.
We are committed to protecting children's privacy and complying with:
•	UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)
•	EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
•	UK Age Appropriate Design Code
•	Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) principles
•	Google Play Families Policy
•	Apple Kids Category Requirements
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information.
________________________________________
2. Personal Information We Collect from Children
We minimise data collection.
Information that may be entered by a parent:
Parents may optionally enter:
•	Child’s first name
This information:
•	Is stored locally on the device only
•	Is not transmitted to us
•	Is not stored on external servers
•	Is not shared with third parties
We do not have access to this information.
________________________________________
Information collected automatically
We may collect limited anonymous technical information, including:
•	Device type
•	Operating system version
•	App performance data
•	Crash reports
This information:
•	Does not identify the child
•	Cannot be linked to a specific individual
________________________________________
3. How We Use the Information
We use information only to:
•	Provide and operate the app
•	Personalise the experience using the locally stored child name
•	Maintain and improve app performance
•	Fix bugs and crashes
We do not use children's personal information for:
•	Behavioural advertising
•	Profiling
•	Marketing
________________________________________
4. Information Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share children's personal information.
We may share limited anonymous technical data with service providers strictly for:
•	Crash reporting
•	App performance monitoring
•	Showing non-personalised advertisements in the Parents Area only
These providers are required to protect children's privacy.
We do not share the child's name.
________________________________________
5. Advertising
The app includes a Parents Area protected by a parental gate.
Advertising:
•	Is shown only in the Parents Area
•	Is intended for parents, not children
•	Is non-personalised
•	Does not use behavioural tracking
•	Does not use the child’s name
Children cannot access advertising without parental interaction.
________________________________________
6. Parental Consent and Verification
Because the only personal information (child’s name) is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted or collected by Spoons3D, verifiable parental consent is not required for data collection by us.
However:
•	Only parents or guardians should enter the child’s name
•	The Parents Area is protected by a parental gate designed to prevent child access
________________________________________
7. Parents’ Rights
Parents and guardians have the right to:
•	Review their child's information
•	Delete their child's information
•	Stop further collection of information
Because the child's name is stored locally only, parents can delete it directly within the app or by uninstalling the app.
Parents may also contact us at:
spoons3d@outlook.com
We will respond within 30 days.
________________________________________
8. Data Retention Policy
We retain anonymous technical data only as long as necessary to:
•	Maintain app functionality
•	Improve performance
•	Fix errors
The child’s name is stored locally and remains until:
•	The parent deletes it, or
•	The app is uninstalled
We do not retain personal child data on our servers.
________________________________________
9. Data Security
We implement reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, including:
•	Secure storage practices
•	Limiting data collection
•	Preventing unauthorised access
Because personal data is not transmitted to our servers, risk is minimised.

These services must comply with applicable privacy laws.
